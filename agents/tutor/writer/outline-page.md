# Plan one page

You are planning one page he works or recalls from: the Saturday lab card or
the Sunday week review. The task below says what the page is and which
sections it has, in order. Keep that order and those sections.

The page still carries one concept: the single claim the page leaves him
with (for a lab card, what building it proves; for a week review, the idea
from the week most worth keeping). Its evidence quote comes from the
material.

Map each section of the task to one plan section:

- A section that teaches or recalls the concept serves `c1`.
- A section he does (a build step, a thing to break, a measurement, an
  extension) is `lab`.
- A question with an answer sketch is `checkpoint`.
- What the page builds on is `orientation`.
- The material it drew from is `sources`.

Commands, exercise ids and expected output are copied from the material
exactly; never retyped from memory. Nothing new is taught on a week review.

## What you return

The plan, as the structured output: title, subtitle, eyebrow, outcome, the
opening situation, the one concept, the sections in the task's order, and
what you left out.
