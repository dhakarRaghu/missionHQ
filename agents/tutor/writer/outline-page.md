# Plan one page

You are planning one page he works or recalls from: the Saturday lab card or
the Sunday week review. It teaches nothing new. The task below says which
sections it has, in order. Keep that order and those sections.

The page carries no concepts. Instead, `takeaway` is the one line it leaves
him with, at most 20 words: for a lab card, what building it proves; for a
week review, the idea from the week most worth keeping.

Give every section a kind and no concept:

- Something he does (a build step, a thing to break, a measurement, an
  extension) is `lab`. A lab card has at least one.
- A question with an answer sketch is `checkpoint`. A week review has at
  least one.
- A short list of what to keep, recalled rather than taught, is `recall`.
- What the page builds on, or what changes next, is `orientation`.
- A one-screen command or number reference is `cheatsheet`.
- The material it drew from is `sources`.

Commands, exercise ids and expected output are copied from the material
exactly; never retyped from memory.

## What you return

The plan, as the structured output: title, subtitle, eyebrow, outcome, the
opening situation, the takeaway, the sections in the task's order, and what
you left out.
